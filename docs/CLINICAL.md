# Clinical basis

## Screening

| Instrument | Use in Dhriti | Scoring |
|---|---|---|
| **OCI-R** (Foa et al., 2002) | First-stage screen, 18 items read aloud | 0-4 per item, total 0-72; **≥ 21 suggests OCD** (Foa et al. 2002 recommended cut-off). Six subscales (washing, checking, ordering, obsessing, hoarding, neutralising), 0-12 each, give the symptom profile. |
| **Y-BOCS, self-report** (Goodman et al., 1989; Baer et al., 1993) | Severity, 10 items with item-specific options | Total 0-40: 0-7 subclinical, 8-15 mild, 16-23 moderate, 24-31 severe, 32-40 extreme. |
| **PHQ-2 + PHQ-9 item 9** (Kroenke et al., 2003; public domain) | Mood check: depression often co-occurs with OCD | PHQ-2 ≥ 3 raises care to at least level 3; any item-9 answer > 0 opens crisis support and sets level 4. |

The clinician-rated Y-BOCS is a semi-structured interview; an app can only administer the self-report form. Every result is labelled **"screening, not a diagnosis"**. The Hindi item wording is the project's working translation; a validated translation must be used for clinical deployment.

## Stepped care (care levels)

| Level | When | Dhriti does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Wellbeing | OCI-R < 21 or Y-BOCS subclinical | Lessons, calming tools |
| 2 Guided self-help | Mild (8-15) | Self-guided ERP, ladder, 360° scenes |
| 3 Therapist-supported | Moderate (16-23) | Refers to a psychiatrist or RCI clinical psychologist; app supports between sessions |
| 4 Psychiatrist first | Severe / extreme (24+) | Psychiatrists only; no self-led exposure |

**Treatment response** is reported as a ≥ 35% reduction in Y-BOCS (Mataix-Cols et al., 2016 consensus). A 2024 individual-participant meta-analysis suggested ≥ 30% may be optimal; we keep the consensus value and note this.

## ERP rules built into the product

- **No reassurance**, in any form: direct answers, facts, statistics, "general" statements ("thoughts don't mean you'll act"), teachings.
- **No accommodation or compromise:** no "just a wipe", "quick rinse", "one prayer".
- **No avoidance:** never suggest distance from a person or removing the trigger in response to an intrusive thought.
- **No proxy:** never send the question to a partner, relative or priest; suggest asking for company instead.
- **Function over content:** intrusive thoughts are unwanted, resisted and neutralised; an emergency is an act, an injury or a wanted intention with a plan.
- **Name it:** "this is the OCD asking for reassurance", then restate the plan and ground (slow out-breath, let anxiety rise and fall).
- **Meditation without neutralising:** thought labelling, never a chant to cancel a thought.

## Professionals (KYC)

- Psychiatrists: MBBS + MD/DNB/DPM in Psychiatry, registered with the National Medical Commission or a State Medical Council.
- Clinical psychologists: registered with the Rehabilitation Council of India (RCI); M.Phil Clinical Psychology or RCI-recognised equivalent.
- Every application is reviewed by a human against the public register before patients can see it.
- Teleconsultation must follow India's Telemedicine Practice Guidelines (2020).

## Safety

- Self-harm → full-screen support with Tele-MANAS 14416 / 1-800-891-4416, trusted contact and 112.
- Emergencies (an act or injury) → 108 / 112.
- Panic attacks are distinct from OCD and are handled by the voice panic mode.
